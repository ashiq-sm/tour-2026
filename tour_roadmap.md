## ⚠️ Tour Code of Conduct: Do's and Don'ts (By Argho)
### ✅ Do's (অবশ্যই পালনীয়):
1. **Strictly follow the schedule:** Don't waste unnecessary time.
2. **Permission & Teamwork:** Don't go anywhere without informing someone or a respected teacher/sir. Never wander off alone.
3. **Keep the bag light:** Essential for hillside trekking in Mirinja and ship transfers.
4. **Hydration:** Always keep a bottle of drinking water in your backpack.
5. **Emergency funds:** Keep extra cash (৳3,000–৳4,000) for medical or remote island emergencies.
6. **Socks for Coral:** Carry protective socks for walking on sharp dead corals in Saint Martin.

### ❌ Don'ts (যা একদমই করবেন না):
1. **No High Heels:** We have to trek through hill slopes and coral shores; wear comfortable grip sandals or sneakers (unless you wanna tweak your ankle/s).
2. **No disruptive behavior:** Don't do anything or draw unwanted attention that may potentially hamper the tour.
3. **No plastics / beach disruption:** Strict government restrictions on loud speakers, bright lights, and single-use polythene on the island.
